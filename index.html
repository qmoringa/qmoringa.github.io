Our community

A warm welcome to Crystal Key

Whether you’re a longtime resident, a new neighbor, or simply exploring our community, we’re glad you’re here. Our family-friendly, gated community of 83 single-family homes in Boynton Beach, FL, is just minutes from South Florida’s beaches, with shops and restaurants a short drive away. Easy access to I-95 puts the West Palm Beach airport 20 minutes away, Fort Lauderdale 30 minutes, and Miami 60 minutes.

We invite our residents to visit our Resident Portal, your convenient connection to the association’s online services. Just sign in to get started. We’ll be sharing community news and important updates there, so we hope you’ll stop by often.
