Our community

A warm welcome to Crystal Key

Our family-friendly, gated community of 83 homes in Boynton Beach, FL, is just minutes from beautiful South Florida beaches. With easy access to I-95, we are 20 minutes from the West Palm Beach airport, 30 minutes from Fort Lauderdale, and 60 minutes from Miami. Shops and restaurants are a short drive away. Whether you’re a longtime resident, a new neighbor, or simply exploring our community, we’re so glad you’re here.

We invite you to visit our Resident Portal, your convenient connection to the association’s online services. Just sign in to get started. We’ll be sharing community news and important updates there, so we hope you’ll stop by often.
